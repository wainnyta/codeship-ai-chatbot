# NimbusStack support policy

*Company-wide document, updated 2026-04-01*

## Priority definitions

- **P1:** the service is down or unusable for all users of a workspace.
- **P2:** a core feature is degraded or unavailable for some users, with a workaround.
- **P3:** a question, or a minor defect with no operational impact.
- **P4:** a feature request.

## Business hours

Business hours are 08:00 to 18:00 in the customer's contracted time zone, Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays in that jurisdiction. Where an SLA says "24x7", the clock runs continuously.

## Support plans

Each product's pricing table names the support plan for each tier. Response times are in each product's own SLA table; they differ by product.
