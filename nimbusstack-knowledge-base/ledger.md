# Nimbus Ledger

*Product documentation, updated 2026-06-30*

Nimbus Ledger meters usage and produces invoices for usage-based pricing.

## Features

- Usage metering from events or from a CSV import
- Rating rules: tiers, volume discounts, minimum commitments
- Invoice generation and dunning
- Federated login (SAML 2.0) in every tier

## Pricing

Prices are per month on an annual plan, plus 0.5% of invoiced volume.

|  | Starter | Pro | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
